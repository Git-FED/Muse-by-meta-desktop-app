02 / Quick answers

Before you
send the note.

Short answers for the questions that tend to slow a good conversation down.

What should I include?

Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and what a useful outcome would look like. A screenshot or link helps, but clarity beats ceremony.

Can I send a half-formed idea?

Yes. Half-formed is often where the useful edge is hiding. Send the context you have and we will help shape the next step.

What if I am not sure this is support?

Send the note anyway. We would rather route a real question than make you figure out the right door before you knock.
